# Ambit Marks (DRAFT, not in effect)

Status: **draft. Not approved by the marks owner.** No badge or mark is issued
under this document, and nothing here establishes third-party verification,
certification or a security guarantee.

## Owner

- Proposed owner: **ond.ai** (proposed, not yet confirmed as the approving owner).
  Ownership of a name is separate from the website that hosts its rules.
- Authoritative policy and brand-rules URL: **pending verification**. The
  README historically linked `ond.cc/ambit/verified` and `ond.cc/ambit/brand`.
  Those pages have not been confirmed to host an effective, approved policy.
  They are kept as historical references only, and must not be treated as the
  policy until verified.
- Pinned URL, commit or version of the brand rules: **pending**.

## Permitted claims (proposed)

- **"Ambit tested"** may be used only as a self-test label. A claim using it
  must name the exact specification revision, profiles, Bench suite version and
  hash, implementation identity, passed, failed and unsupported cases, and who
  evaluated it, as required by CORE-028 in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md). The label must
  be presented as self-reported.
- **"Ambit verified"** is reserved. No criteria, issuer, independence rule or
  listing process has been adopted, so it must not be used.
- No label may be described as certification, a security guarantee, conformance
  with a future stable release, or verification by a third party, unless a
  marks policy adopted by the owner says so.

## Self-test versus independent test

- A self-test is run and published by the implementer. It proves only what its
  published report shows.
- An independent test would be run by a party other than the implementer. The
  independence criteria, reviewer requirements and handling of conflicts are
  **not yet defined**. Until they are, outcomes must be labelled internal or
  deferred (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), "Review and Versioning").

- Request and listing process: **pending**.
- Revocation triggers (for example, a changed implementation, a failed
